# Hearth & Hird artwork

These assets were created specifically for Hearth & Hird and do not reuse the
VikingSettlements “VS” badge or official Valheim branding.

## Hero

Files:

- `hearth-and-hird-hero-1600.jpg`
- `hearth-and-hird-hero-800.jpg`

Final prompt:

> Original cinematic key art for Hearth & Hird showing both pillars of the
> mod: a living Norse settlement and a disciplined player-led warband. A
> rugged Scandinavian valley at golden-hour dawn, with a structurally
> believable longhall, turf-roof homes, central hearth, palisade, watchtower,
> farms and settlers physically chopping, carrying timber and tending crops.
> In the foreground, a cloaked leader commands shield-bearers and archers.
> Premium hand-painted game concept art with grounded stylised realism, warm
> firelight against cool Nordic mist, historically grounded equipment, no
> text, logos, watermarks, horned helmets, floating weapons or impossible
> buildings.

## Emblem

Files:

- `hearth-and-hird-icon-256.png`
- `VikingSettlements/Package/icon.png`

Final prompt:

> A bold original Norse emblem combining a central hearth flame, longhouse
> roof, round iron-and-oak shield and two upright spears. Symmetrical,
> legible at 32 pixels, dark pine green, aged oak, charcoal iron, muted brass
> and ember orange. No text, initials, runes, “VS”, official Valheim logo,
> watermark, signature or horned helmet.

The original large generations were cropped only to remove empty edge space
and resized for repository/package use.
